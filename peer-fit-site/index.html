Find a game in 4 steps

See what's being played near you, check the details, and reserve your place.

Swipe to see every step →

Step 1

Open Discover

Tap Discover in the bottom menu. You can also search by event or location.

Step 2

Filter your search

Choose your level, the date and the sport.

Step 3

Check the details

Date, duration, level, intensity and places left. Tap the address for directions.

Step 4

Join

Tap Join to reserve your place. Your next session starts here.

Host your own game

Set it up once and let players come to you.

Swipe to see every step →

Step 1

Open My Events

Tap My Events, then the + button.

Step 2

Add the basics

Give your event a clear title, choose the sport and the skill level.

Step 3

Choose the sport

Search the list, or add a custom sport if yours isn't there.

Step 4

Set the details

Max players and intensity. Free or paid, open or private.

Step 5

Review & publish

Check every detail, then tap Publish event.

Step 6

Manage your event

Find it under Created. Chat with players or update the event.
